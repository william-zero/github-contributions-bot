# Random Facts That Will Ruin Small Talk

## The Moon is Slowly Ghosting Earth
The Moon drifts about 3.8 cm farther from Earth each year. In roughly 600 million years, total solar eclipses will no longer be possible — the Moon will appear too small to fully cover the Sun. We are living in a cosmologically rare window of perfect eclipses.

## Cleopatra Lived Closer in Time to the Moon Landing Than to the Pyramids
Cleopatra lived around 69–30 BCE. The Great Pyramid of Giza was completed around 2560 BCE. The Moon landing was 1969 CE. That means the Moon landing is about 1,000 years *closer* in time to Cleopatra than the pyramids are.

## Trees Are Mostly Air
About 95% of a tree's mass comes from CO₂ pulled out of the air, not from the soil. The soil provides minerals and water, but the carbon backbone of wood is literally made from air molecules. Trees are, in a sense, solidified sky.
