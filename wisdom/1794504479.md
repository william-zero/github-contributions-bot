# Daily Facts: The Surprising World of Sleep

**Fact 1:** Humans spend roughly one-third of their lives asleep — that's about 26 years for an average lifespan. During that time, the brain processes memories, the body repairs tissue, and absolutely nobody knows where your socks go.

**Fact 2:** Otters hold hands while sleeping so they don't drift apart. This behavior is called "rafting" and is simultaneously the most adorable thing in nature and a reasonable relationship goal.
