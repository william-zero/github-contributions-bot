# The Surprisingly Social Life of Trees

Trees in forests communicate through underground fungal networks nicknamed the "Wood Wide Web." A mother tree can recognize its own seedlings and send them extra nutrients through these mycorrhizal networks, giving them a head start in life.

Old-growth trees can live for thousands of years — the oldest known individual tree is a bristlecone pine in California named Methuselah, estimated to be over 4,800 years old, predating the Egyptian pyramids.
