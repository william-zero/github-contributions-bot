# Surprising Facts About Numbers

- The number 40 is the only number whose letters appear in alphabetical order (f-o-r-t-y).
- If you shuffle a standard deck of cards, the exact order has almost certainly never existed before in human history — there are 8×10^67 possible arrangements.
- A "jiffy" is an actual unit of time: 1/100th of a second in computing, or the time light takes to travel 1 centimeter in physics.
