# Today's Slightly Unsettling Facts

## Fact 1: Cleopatra is Closer to the Moon Landing Than to the Great Pyramid
Cleopatra lived around 69–30 BC. The Great Pyramid was built around 2560 BC. The Moon landing was in 1969.
That means Cleopatra is roughly 2,491 years from the pyramid and only 1,999 years from Apollo 11.
Cleopatra is literally more modern than the pyramids. Let that rearrange your brain.

## Fact 2: Mammoths Were Alive When the Great Pyramid Was Built
A small population of woolly mammoths survived on Wrangel Island in the Arctic until about 1650 BC —
centuries after the pharaohs built Giza. The Egyptians had already invented writing and beer while
mammoths were still wandering around on an island. Coexistence is underrated.
