# Fun Facts: Numbers That Break Your Brain

- The number 0.999... (repeating) is mathematically equal to 1, not just "close to" 1. This can be proven multiple ways and mathematicians universally agree — it's not an approximation.
- A googolplex (10^googol) is so large that writing it out would require more space than exists in the observable universe, even if each digit were one Planck length wide.
