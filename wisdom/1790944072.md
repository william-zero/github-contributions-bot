# Fun Facts for Today

## The Doorstep Mile
When you're about to arrive home after a long journey, the last mile feels the longest. Psychologists call this the "doorstep mile" effect — anticipation amplifies perceived time, making the final stretch feel like it stretches forever.

## Competitive Altruism
Humans are the only known species that compete to be MORE generous than others. In some cultures, a phenomenon called "competitive altruism" emerges where people try to outgive each other in a kind of generosity arms race.
