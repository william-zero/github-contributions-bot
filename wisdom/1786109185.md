# Today's Wisdom

**The Honey Never Spoils:** Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Honey's low moisture content and naturally acidic pH create an inhospitable environment for bacteria — it essentially preserves itself indefinitely.

**Wombat Cubes:** Wombats are the only animals known to produce cubic feces. Their digestive tract has unique elasticity that shapes droppings into cubes, which stack neatly and don't roll away — useful for marking territory on flat surfaces.
