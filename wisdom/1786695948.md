# Things That Are Surprisingly Loud (When You Think About Them)

- A blue whale's call can reach 188 decibels — louder than a jet engine — and can travel hundreds of miles underwater. Effectively, whales have been doing long-distance phone calls since before phones existed.

- The pistol shrimp snaps its claw so fast it creates a cavitation bubble that briefly reaches the temperature of the sun's surface. It's a tiny shrimp. Running a sun. In an ocean. For no particular reason.

- Silence is technically impossible: the quietest place on Earth (Microsoft's anechoic chamber) is so quiet you can hear your own heartbeat, blood flow, and the sound of your nervous system conducting electricity. Scientists reportedly go slightly mad after spending too long in there.
