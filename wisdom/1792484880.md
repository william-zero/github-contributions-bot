# Fun Facts: Animal Superpowers

## Mantis Shrimp Vision
Humans have 3 types of color receptors (red, green, blue). Mantis shrimp have 16 types, letting them perceive colors invisible to humans — including ultraviolet and infrared wavelengths. Ironically, research suggests they may be worse at distinguishing similar colors than humans are.

## Axolotl Regeneration
The axolotl salamander can regrow not just limbs, but also parts of its brain, heart, and spinal cord. Scientists are still studying the exact mechanism, hoping it might unlock regenerative therapies for humans. Axolotls never actually metamorphose — they stay in a permanent larval state.
