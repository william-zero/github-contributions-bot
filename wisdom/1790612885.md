# Today's Fun Facts

- The dot over the letters "i" and "j" is called a **tittle**. You've been titillated by linguistics this whole time.
- Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still edible — eternal sweetness, zero updates required.
- A group of cats is called a **clowder**, but a group of wild cats is a **destruction**. Nature named that one correctly.
