# Did You Know?

**Mantis shrimp see 16 types of color receptors** — humans have 3. Scientists believe they don't actually process more colors than us, they just process them differently, like 16-channel hardware running simpler firmware.

**A group of crows is called a murder**, but a group of ravens is called an unkindness. Whoever was naming bird flocks in the Middle Ages was clearly having a rough time.
