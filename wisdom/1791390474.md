# Today's Weird Facts

- The average cloud weighs about 1.1 million pounds (500,000 kg), yet floats because the water droplets are so tiny and spread out that the density is less than the surrounding air.
- A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance." A group of owls is called a "parliament." A group of crows is called a "murder." Ornithologists were clearly having a great time naming things.
