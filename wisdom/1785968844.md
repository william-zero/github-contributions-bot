## Random Facts for Today
- Octopuses have three hearts: two pump blood to the gills, and one pumps it to the rest of the body. When an octopus swims, the heart delivering blood to the body actually stops beating — which is why they prefer crawling.
- The surface of the Sun is about 5,500°C (9,932°F), but the corona (the outer atmosphere) reaches temperatures exceeding 1,000,000°C — a phenomenon still not fully explained by scientists.
