# Today's Useless But Delightful Facts

## The Mantis Shrimp's Punch
Mantis shrimp strike with the force of a bullet — accelerating their clubs at 10,000 g. The impact creates a cavitation bubble that collapses with a flash of light and a shockwave nearly as destructive as the punch itself. They are, in effect, punching twice with one punch.

## Ants and Collective Intelligence
A single ant has a brain with roughly 250,000 neurons and behaves somewhat randomly. A colony of a million ants has effectively 250 billion neurons' worth of emergent coordination — building temperature-regulated nests, farming fungi, and waging organized wars. The colony is smarter than the ant, in the same way a brain is smarter than a neuron.
