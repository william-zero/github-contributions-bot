# Strange Facts About Everyday Things

**Honey Never Spoils**
Archaeologists have discovered 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Honey's low moisture content, acidic pH, and natural hydrogen peroxide production make it inhospitable to bacteria. A sealed jar of honey has an essentially infinite shelf life.

**The Eiffel Tower Grows in Summer**
Metal expands when heated. The Eiffel Tower, made of iron, can grow up to 15 centimeters (about 6 inches) taller during hot summer days compared to cold winter ones. Engineers accounted for this thermal expansion in the original design.
