# Today's Curiosities

## The Paradox of the Most Common Number
Benford's Law states that in many naturally occurring datasets — financial records, population sizes, street addresses — the leading digit is 1 about 30% of the time, not the expected 11%. The distribution follows a logarithmic curve, with 9 appearing as the first digit less than 5% of the time. Forensic accountants use this law to detect fraud, because fabricated numbers tend to distribute too uniformly.

## You Are Mostly Emptiness
If you removed all the empty space from atoms in the human body, the remaining matter would fit in a cube about 1/500th the size of a sugar cube — roughly 0.002 mm on a side. Yet that sugar-cube-fraction contains all your mass. The electrons, protons, and neutrons are fantastically tiny relative to the atomic orbits they inhabit — an atom is about 99.9999999999999% empty space.
