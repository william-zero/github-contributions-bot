# Weekend Fact: Tardigrade Resilience

Tardigrades (water bears) can survive the vacuum of outer space by entering a state called cryptobiosis — they essentially pause all metabolic processes and can remain dormant for decades. When rehydrated, they wake up like nothing happened.

They've been to space on multiple missions and some survived unprotected exposure to vacuum and radiation. Nature invented a biological pause button millions of years before science fiction thought of it.
