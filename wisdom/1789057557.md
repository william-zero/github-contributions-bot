# Daily Facts

## The Pirahã Language Has No Numbers
The Pirahã people of the Amazon have a language with no words for numbers at all — not even "one" or "two." They use terms roughly meaning "a small amount" and "a larger amount." Linguist Daniel Everett spent decades with them and concluded they simply don't need counting in their hunter-gatherer lifestyle.

## Hot Water Can Freeze Faster Than Cold Water
Under certain conditions, hot water freezes faster than cold water — a phenomenon called the Mpemba effect (yes, it was rediscovered, but the name stuck from a 1969 study). While scientists still debate the exact mechanism, it has been reliably observed in experiments involving identical containers and identical freezing environments.
