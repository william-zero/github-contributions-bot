## Competitive Eating: An Olympic-Adjacent Sport

Takeru Kobayashi ate 50 hot dogs in 12 minutes in 2001, effectively quadrupling the
world record at the time. His secret? Standing while eating, a "Solomon method" of
breaking the dog in half, and dunking the buns in water to reduce volume.

The sport now has governing bodies, prize money, and talent scouts. The hot dogs remain
unchanged.
