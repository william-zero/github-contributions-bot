# Numbers That Break Your Brain

- There are more possible iterations of a game of chess than there are atoms in the observable universe
- 111,111,111 × 111,111,111 = 12,345,678,987,654,321
- If you shuffle a deck of cards properly, the order is almost certainly unique — the same order has never existed before in history
- A jiffy is an actual unit of time: 1/100th of a second (or sometimes 1/60th in electronics)
- Googol (10^100) is so large that writing it out would require more paper than the observable universe could hold
