# Fun Facts

## The Bermuda Triangle Is Statistically Boring
Despite its fearsome reputation, Lloyd's of London — the insurance market that should know — does not charge higher premiums for ships passing through the Bermuda Triangle. Statistically, it is no more dangerous than any other heavily trafficked stretch of ocean. The "mystery" is largely a product of selective reporting: incidents elsewhere get forgotten while Bermuda Triangle incidents get sensationalized.

## Octopuses Have Three Hearts
Two branchial hearts pump blood through the gills; a third systemic heart pumps it to the rest of the body. When an octopus swims, the systemic heart stops beating — which is why they prefer crawling. Swimming literally tires them out at a cardiac level.
