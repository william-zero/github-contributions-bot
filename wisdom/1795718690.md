# Fun Facts

## Mantis Shrimp Vision
Mantis shrimp have 16 types of photoreceptors compared to humans' 3, yet they're actually worse at distinguishing colors — they process color locally rather than comparatively, like tiny biological spectrometers.

## The Smell of Rain
The earthy scent after rain (petrichor) comes from geosmin, a compound produced by soil bacteria. Humans can detect geosmin at concentrations as low as 5 parts per trillion — we're more sensitive to it than sharks are to blood.
