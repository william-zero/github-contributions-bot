## Today's Curious Facts

**The Elevator Paradox:** If you're waiting for an elevator, it seems like it always arrives going the wrong direction. This is actually mathematically true — elevators spend more time at the extremes (ground floor, top floor), so you're statistically more likely to intercept one heading away from your destination.

**Knocking on Wood:** The superstition of knocking on wood to ward off bad luck may originate from pagan beliefs that spirits lived in trees, and knocking would either wake them to help or keep them from hearing your boast. Others think it comes from touching relics of the "True Cross" in medieval churches.
