# Fun Facts: Bizarre World Records

- The world's longest hiccuping streak lasted 68 years — Charles Osborne hiccuped an estimated 430 million times from 1922 to 1990, and still managed to live to age 96.
- The shortest war in history lasted just 38–45 minutes: the Anglo-Zanzibar War of 1896, in which Zanzibar surrendered to Britain before most people had finished breakfast.
