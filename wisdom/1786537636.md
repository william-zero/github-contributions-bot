# Deeply Unnecessary But Entirely True Facts

**The shortest war in history lasted 38–45 minutes.** The Anglo-Zanzibar War of 1896 began at 9:02 AM and ended by 9:40 AM when the sultan's palace was shelled and his forces surrendered. The sultan fled to the German consulate and Zanzibar lost one gunboat. History's most efficient conflict.

**A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance."** This is the most accurate collective noun in the English language. A group of crows is called a "murder," a group of owls is a "parliament," and a group of cats is a "clowder" — but nothing tops flamboyance for sheer descriptive accuracy.

**The Hawaiian alphabet has only 13 letters.** A, E, I, O, U, H, K, L, M, N, P, W, and the ʻokina (ʻ). This means the language achieves remarkable expressiveness from minimal materials, which is honestly a better design philosophy than most programming languages.
