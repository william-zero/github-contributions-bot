# Fun Facts: Numbers That Break Your Brain

- There are more possible arrangements of a deck of 52 cards than there are atoms on Earth (8×10^67 vs ~10^50).
- If you count one number per second, it would take over 31 years to count to one billion — and 31,000 years for one trillion.
- The number of grains of sand on Earth is roughly 7.5 × 10^18, but there are more stars in the observable universe (10^24).
- Zero is the only number that is neither positive nor negative, but it is even.
