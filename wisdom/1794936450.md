# Fun Facts: Honey and Tardigrades

**Honey never spoils.** Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Its low moisture content and natural hydrogen peroxide make it essentially immortal in a sealed jar.

**Tardigrades (water bears) can survive almost anything.** These microscopic animals can withstand the vacuum of outer space, boiling temperatures, extreme radiation, and even being dried out for decades — then come back to life when rehydrated. They are basically tiny indestructible bears.
