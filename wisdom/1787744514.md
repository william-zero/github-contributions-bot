# Fun Facts: Animals Doing Wild Things

- Crows hold grudges. They remember human faces for years, warn other crows about "dangerous" people, and have been documented bringing gifts to humans who feed them regularly.
- Mantis shrimp can punch with the force of a bullet and see 16 types of color receptors (humans have 3). They're also stomatopods, not shrimp. The name is a lie.
