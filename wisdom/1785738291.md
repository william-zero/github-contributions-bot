# Curious Facts About Sleep

- Humans spend about one-third of their lives asleep — that's roughly 25 years for an average lifespan.
- Some species never fully sleep: dolphins and whales sleep with only half their brain at a time, keeping the other half active to surface for air.
- During REM sleep, your body is essentially paralyzed — this is thought to prevent you from physically acting out your dreams.
- The record for the longest time without sleep is about 11 days (264 hours), set by Randy Gardner in 1964.
- Snails can sleep for up to three years during droughts, entering a hibernation-like state called estivation.
