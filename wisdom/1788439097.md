# Daily Wisdom

## The Ship of Theseus Is Already Solved

The Ship of Theseus paradox — if you replace every plank, is it still the same ship? — seems philosophical until you realize biology answered it long ago. Your body replaces most of its cells over 7-10 years. The "you" reading this is materially different from the "you" of a decade ago. The answer philosophers missed: identity is pattern, not substance. What makes you *you* is the continuous thread of information (memories, habits, personality) that persists through material change, not the atoms themselves.

## Neurons That Fire Together

Hebbian learning (1949) gave us the phrase "neurons that wire together, fire together." What's stranger: the inverse is also true — neurons that never fire together gradually lose their connection. This is why skills atrophy: unused pathways literally dissolve. Every time you practice something, you're not just performing it — you're voting for its survival in the biological parliament of your brain.
