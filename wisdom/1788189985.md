## More Astonishing Facts

**The Sahara desert used to be a lush savanna.** About 11,000 years ago, it was covered in grasslands, lakes, and wildlife. A shift in Earth's axial tilt changed monsoon patterns and dried it out — and climate cycles suggest it could turn green again in ~15,000 years.

**There are more possible games of chess than atoms in the observable universe.** The number of possible unique games is estimated at 10^120, making chess combinatorially inexhaustible for any computer we could ever build.
