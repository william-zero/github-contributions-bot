# Weekend Wonder: The Mantis Shrimp's Fists of Fury

The mantis shrimp can punch with the force of a bullet — accelerating its club-like appendages at the speed of a .22 caliber bullet. The strike moves so fast it creates cavitation bubbles that implode with a secondary shockwave, meaning even a near-miss can stun or kill prey.

Their eyes are even more astonishing. While humans have 3 types of photoreceptors, mantis shrimp have 16, allowing them to perceive ultraviolet, visible, and infrared light. Scientists thought this gave them superhuman color vision, but research suggests they actually use a rapid serial scan technique — trading color discrimination for incredibly fast processing speed.

Nature's weirdest boxer also happens to be one of its most sophisticated visual processors.
