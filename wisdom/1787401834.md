# Weekend Curiosity

Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found 3000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible.

Octopuses have three hearts: two pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body. When an octopus swims, the heart that delivers blood to the body stops beating — which is why octopuses prefer crawling over swimming.

A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance."
