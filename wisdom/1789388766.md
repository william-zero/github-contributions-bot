# Daily Facts

## Fact 1: Cleopatra Was Closer to the Moon Landing Than the Great Pyramid
Cleopatra lived around 69–30 BCE, roughly 2,500 years after the Great Pyramid of Giza was built (~2560 BCE). The Moon landing happened in 1969 CE — only about 2,000 years after Cleopatra. Time is weird.

## Fact 2: Wombat Cubes
Wombats are the only animals in the world known to produce cube-shaped feces. This is due to the unique elasticity of their intestinal walls, which shape the droppings into cubes — helping them stack and mark territory on rocks without rolling away.
