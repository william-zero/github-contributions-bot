# Two Truths About Time

**The present moment doesn't exist**: By the time your brain processes a sensation, it's already 80-100ms in the past. You live in a slightly delayed reconstruction of reality — consciousness is a highlight reel, not a live stream.

**Rocks remember time**: Certain zircon crystals found in Australia are 4.4 billion years old — older than Earth's oceans. They formed when the planet was still a molten hellscape, survived everything, and now sit in university lab drawers, quietly older than almost everything around them.
