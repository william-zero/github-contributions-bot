# Curious Facts About Color

- The mantis shrimp has 16 types of color receptors compared to humans' 3, yet scientists believe it may actually process color *less* sophisticatedly than we do — it uses color as a simple matching system rather than for mixing and comparing.
- The color orange was named after the fruit, not the other way around. Before the 16th century, English speakers called the color "yellow-red" (geoluhread in Old English).
