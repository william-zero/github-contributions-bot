# Today's Odd Facts

## The Immortal Jellyfish
The *Turritopsis dohrnii* jellyfish is biologically immortal — when stressed or aging, it can revert back to its juvenile polyp state and start its life cycle over again. It is the only known animal capable of repeatedly reverting to sexual immaturity. Essentially, it cheats death every time.

## Competitive Sleeping
There is an annual World Sleep Day, but Finland once held a competition called the "Finnish Sleeping Championships" where participants were judged on the quality and duration of their nap. The winner received a mattress and eternal bragging rights in a country famous for its saunas and stoic silence.
