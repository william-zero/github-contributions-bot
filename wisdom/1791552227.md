# Fun Facts: The Deep Ocean & Bioluminescence

- **95% of Earth's oceans remain unexplored.** The deep sea is so dark and pressurized that most of it has never been seen by human eyes — we know more about the surface of Mars.
- **Bioluminescence is the most common form of communication on Earth.** An estimated 76% of deep-sea animals produce their own light, making the dark ocean a secret light show of living lanterns.
