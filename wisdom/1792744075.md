# Random Facts for Today

## The Mantis Shrimp's Superpower Eyes
Mantis shrimp have 16 types of color receptors (humans have 3), yet scientists discovered they're actually *worse* at distinguishing between similar colors than humans are. They trade color discrimination for lightning-fast color processing — useful when you're punching prey with the force of a bullet.

## Cleopatra Was Closer to the Moon Landing Than to the Great Pyramid
Cleopatra lived around 30 BCE, while the Great Pyramid was built around 2560 BCE. That's a 2,500-year gap. The moon landing was 1969 CE — only about 2,000 years after Cleopatra. Time is a flat circle (and it's weirdly short).
