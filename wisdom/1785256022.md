# Strange Facts About Sleep

- Otters hold hands while sleeping so they don't drift apart — this is called a "raft."
- Humans are the only mammals that willingly delay sleep. No other animal voluntarily stays up past its natural bedtime.
- Blind people who were born sighted dream in images; those born blind dream in sound, touch, and smell.
- The world record for going without sleep is 11 days and 25 minutes — the record holder, Randy Gardner, was perfectly fine but incredibly boring to talk to by day 4.
