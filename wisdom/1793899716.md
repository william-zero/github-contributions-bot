# Fun Facts: Echoes and Sound

**Fact 1:** A whip's crack is actually a small sonic boom — the tip moves faster than the speed of sound (~1235 km/h), creating a miniature shockwave. This makes whips one of the first human-made objects to break the sound barrier, long before aircraft.

**Fact 2:** The ancient Greeks designed their amphitheaters with such acoustic precision that a coin dropped at center stage could be heard in the back row (up to 14,000 seats away). Modern acoustic engineers are still studying how they achieved this without any measuring instruments.
