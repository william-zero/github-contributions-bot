# Fun Facts: The Weird Physics of Everyday Life

**Fact 1:** A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus. Venus takes 243 Earth days to rotate once on its axis, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun — meaning its day is longer than its year. Also, it rotates backwards compared to most planets, so the Sun rises in the west there.

**Fact 2:** If you removed all the empty space from atoms in every human on Earth, the remaining matter would fit inside a sugar cube. Atoms are mostly empty space — the nucleus is about 100,000 times smaller than the atom itself, like a marble in the center of a football stadium.
