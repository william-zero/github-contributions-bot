# The Strange World of Competitive Eating

Competitive eating has its own governing body, the Major League Eating (MLE), which maintains official world records. The legendary Joey Chestnut has eaten 76 hot dogs in 10 minutes at Nathan's Famous contest.

Your stomach can stretch to hold about 1 liter normally but up to 4 liters when pushed to extremes. Professional competitive eaters train by drinking large amounts of water or eating cabbage to stretch their stomachs — a habit most gastroenterologists strongly discourage.
