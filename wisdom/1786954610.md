# Things That Are Strangely Symmetrical

- A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance" — and they must eat with their heads upside-down.
- The word "racecar" reads the same forwards and backwards, and a racecar engine fires cylinders in a palindromic order.
- Honeybees build hexagonal cells because hexagons are the most efficient shape for packing — nature did the math before humans did.
