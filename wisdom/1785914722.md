# Curious Facts About Fungi

- Fungi are more closely related to animals than to plants — they share a common ancestor and even have similar cell wall chemistry (chitin) found in insect exoskeletons.
- The largest living organism on Earth is a honey fungus (*Armillaria ostoyae*) in Oregon's Blue Mountains, spanning about 3.5 square miles and estimated to be over 8,000 years old.
- Fungi communicate through electrical signals similar to nerve impulses, and some researchers have even mapped these signals onto human language patterns.
