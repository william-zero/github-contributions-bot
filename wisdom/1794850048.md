## Fun Fact: Crows Hold Grudges

Crows can recognize individual human faces and remember people who have wronged them for years. They've been observed dive-bombing specific people while ignoring others in the same area — holding grudges longer than most exes.

## Fun Fact: Trees Talk Underground

Trees in forests communicate and share nutrients through underground fungal networks called the "Wood Wide Web." Older "mother trees" can send carbon to struggling seedlings through these mycelium highways, essentially feeding their forest children.
