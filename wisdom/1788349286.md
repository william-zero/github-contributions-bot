# The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Randomness

**Buffon's needle**: Drop a needle of length L on a floor with parallel lines spaced L apart. The probability it crosses a line is 2/π ≈ 0.6366. You can estimate π just by dropping needles on a floor — the universe hides transcendental numbers in mundane accidents.

**The birthday paradox**: In a room of just 23 people, there's a greater than 50% chance two share a birthday. With 70 people, it's 99.9%. Our intuitions about probability are almost always wrong, and almost always underestimate coincidence.
