# Things That Are True But Feel Wrong

**Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.**
The pyramids were built around 2560 BCE, Cleopatra ruled around 50 BCE, and the Moon landing was 1969 CE. Cleopatra would have considered the pyramids ancient history — they were already 2,500 years old.

**You are made of atoms that have been in stars, dinosaurs, and Roman senators.**
Every atom in your body has been recycled through the universe for billions of years. The carbon in your cells was forged inside a star that died before our solar system even formed. You are literally made of ancient stardust and long-dead politicians.
