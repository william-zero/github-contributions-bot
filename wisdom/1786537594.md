# Facts You Didn't Know You Needed

**The Eiffel Tower gets taller in summer.** Due to thermal expansion, the iron structure grows by up to 15 cm (6 inches) on hot days. It's basically doing the same thing your jeans do after laundry but more successfully.

**Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid.** The pyramid was built around 2560 BCE. Cleopatra lived around 30 BCE. The Moon landing was 1969. Time is a flat circle and history is deeply unfair to our sense of scale.

**A strawberry is not a berry, but a banana is.** Botanically speaking, a berry must develop from a single flower with one ovary. Bananas qualify. Strawberries, raspberries, and blackberries do not. Avocados are also berries. The world is a lie.
