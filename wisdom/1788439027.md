# Daily Wisdom

## The Mantis Shrimp's Rainbow Vision

Mantis shrimp have 16 types of color receptors (humans only have 3), yet studies show they're actually worse at distinguishing similar colors than humans. Their eyes are tuned for super-fast identification of specific hues rather than fine color discrimination — basically the opposite of what everyone assumed. They trade nuance for speed, seeing a world sorted into bins rather than a spectrum.

## The Library of Babel Paradox

If you had a library containing every possible book — every combination of letters across 410-page volumes — it would contain both the complete works of Shakespeare and an infinite number of books with one character wrong. It would also contain every true book and every false book, every prophecy fulfilled and unfulfilled. The philosopher Jorge Luis Borges imagined such a place and noted: the library is useless, not because it lacks information, but because it has too much.
