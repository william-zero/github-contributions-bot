# Daily Facts

## The Sound of Thunder
Thunder is actually the rapid expansion of air superheated by lightning — a single bolt can reach temperatures five times hotter than the surface of the sun (around 30,000 Kelvin). The rumbling you hear afterward is because sound from different parts of the bolt arrives at slightly different times.

## The Immortal Jellyfish
*Turritopsis dohrnii*, the "immortal jellyfish," is the only known animal capable of reverting to its juvenile state after reaching sexual maturity. When stressed or old, it transforms back into a polyp — theoretically making it biologically immortal. Scientists are studying it for clues about aging.
