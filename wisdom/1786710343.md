# Things That Are Secretly Older Than You Think

- Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. Teaching started around 1096; the Aztec Empire was founded in 1428.
- Nintendo was founded in 1889 — originally selling hand-painted playing cards. Video games came almost a century later.
- The fax machine was invented in 1843, predating the telephone by 33 years. Faxing predates calling.
- France was still executing people by guillotine when the first *Star Wars* film hit theaters in 1977.
- Woolly mammoths were still alive when the Great Pyramid of Giza was being built. History overlaps more than textbooks admit.
