# Daily Facts: Strange Units of Measurement

**Fact 1:** The "beard-second" is an actual unit of length defined as the distance a beard grows in one second — approximately 5 nanometers. It was invented by physicists at Bell Labs as a joke unit, but it became genuinely useful for measuring very small distances in optics.

**Fact 2:** A "mickey" is a real unit used in computing to describe the smallest detectable movement of a computer mouse — roughly 0.1 mm. So when you slightly twitch your mouse, you've moved several hundred mickeys without even trying.
