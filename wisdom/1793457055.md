# Halloween Facts

- The word "witch" comes from the Old English "wicce," meaning wise woman.
- Jack-o'-lanterns were originally carved from turnips in Ireland, not pumpkins.
- Halloween generates about $10 billion in consumer spending in the US each year, making it the second-largest commercial holiday after Christmas.
- The fear of Halloween is called Samhainophobia, derived from the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain.
- Candy corn was originally called "Chicken Feed" when it was invented in the 1880s.
