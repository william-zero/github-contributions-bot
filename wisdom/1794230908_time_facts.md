# Fun Facts: The Weirdness of Time

- The word "noon" originally meant the ninth hour after sunrise (3 PM by modern reckoning). It gradually shifted to midday over the Middle Ages as monks moved their meal earlier.
- A "moment" was an actual unit of time in medieval Europe: exactly 1/40th of an hour, or 90 seconds.
- Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing (1969) than to the construction of the Great Pyramid (~2560 BCE). The pyramids are older to Cleopatra than Cleopatra is to us.
