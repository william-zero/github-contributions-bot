# Facts That Sound Like Lies But Aren't

- A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance." No one involved in naming this regrets anything.
- Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. When Montezuma was born, Oxford had already been teaching for 300 years.
- Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
- There are more possible iterations of a game of chess than there are atoms in the observable universe.
- Scotland's national animal is the unicorn. This is official. It is on the coat of arms.
