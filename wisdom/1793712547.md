# Fun Facts: Numbers and Patterns

- The number 0 was independently invented in three places: Mesopotamia (~300 BC), India (~458 AD), and Maya civilization (~350 AD). Each culture arrived at the idea of "nothing" on its own, which is either beautiful or deeply philosophical.
- If you shuffle a standard deck of cards perfectly randomly, the exact sequence you get has almost certainly never occurred before in the history of card games — there are 52! possible arrangements (a number with 68 digits).
