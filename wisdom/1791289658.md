# Daily Curious Facts

## Octopus Neurons
Roughly two-thirds of an octopus's neurons are located in its arms, not its brain.
Each arm can taste, touch, and make decisions semi-independently —
like having eight tiny semi-autonomous robots attached to your torso.

## The Turritopsis Dohrnii Jellyfish
When stressed or injured, this jellyfish reverts back to its juvenile polyp stage,
effectively restarting its life cycle. Scientists call this transdifferentiation,
and it's the closest thing nature has to a biological rewind button.
