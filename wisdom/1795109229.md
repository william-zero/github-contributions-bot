# Daily Facts — $(date +%Y-%m-%d)

**Fact 1:** Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza. The pyramids were built around 2560 BCE, Cleopatra died in 30 BCE (~2530 years later), and the Moon landing was in 1969 CE (~2000 years later).

**Fact 2:** Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Its low moisture content and acidic pH make it essentially antimicrobial.
