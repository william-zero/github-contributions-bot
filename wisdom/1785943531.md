# Curious Facts About Lightning

- A single bolt of lightning is about 5 times hotter than the surface of the sun, reaching roughly 30,000 Kelvin.
- Lightning strikes Earth about 100 times every second, totaling around 8 million strikes per day worldwide.
- The odds of being struck by lightning in your lifetime are roughly 1 in 15,300 — about the same as finding a four-leaf clover on your first try.
