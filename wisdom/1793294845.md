# The Mantis Shrimp's Spectacular Vision

Mantis shrimp have 16 types of photoreceptors — humans only have 3. They can see ultraviolet, visible, and infrared light, plus they can detect polarized light. Ironically, their color *discrimination* is worse than ours; they don't mix colors like we do, they just check which receptor fires.

They also punch with the force of a bullet and can shatter aquarium glass. Truly nature's most chaotic creature.
