# Fun Facts: The Strange World of Time

- A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus — it rotates so slowly that the sun rises in the west and sets in the east.
- The shortest war in history lasted 38 to 45 minutes: the Anglo-Zanzibar War of 1896.
- Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
