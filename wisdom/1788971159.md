# Today's Fun Facts

- Octopuses have three hearts, two of which stop beating when they swim — which is why they prefer crawling. Their blue blood contains hemocyanin, a copper-based molecule that carries oxygen efficiently in cold, low-oxygen ocean environments.

- The word "checkmate" in chess comes from the Persian phrase "shāh māt," meaning "the king is dead" — which is slightly dramatic, since the king isn't actually captured, just thoroughly embarrassed.
