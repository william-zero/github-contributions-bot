# Fact: Tardigrade Survival

Tardigrades — also known as water bears — can survive in the vacuum of outer space. These microscopic animals can enter a cryptobiotic state called "tun," where they expel nearly all water from their bodies and reduce their metabolism to 0.01% of normal. In this state, they can endure temperatures from -272°C to 151°C, radiation hundreds of times lethal to humans, and pressures six times greater than the deepest ocean trenches. Scientists have even revived tardigrades that had been frozen for over 30 years. They may be the most resilient animals on Earth — or beyond it.
