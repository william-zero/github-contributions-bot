# Fun Facts: Bioluminescence and the Deep Sea

- The deep ocean is the largest living space on Earth by volume, yet over 80% of it remains unexplored. Most organisms living below 200 meters have never been catalogued by science.

- Roughly 76% of all deep-sea creatures are bioluminescent — they produce their own light via chemical reactions. This glow serves purposes ranging from predator confusion and prey attraction to surprisingly elaborate mating displays in pitch-black water.
