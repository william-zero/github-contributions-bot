# Fun Facts: Octopuses and Time Zones

## The Octopus Has Three Hearts
An octopus has three hearts: two pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body. When an octopus swims, the heart that delivers blood to the body actually stops beating — which is why they prefer crawling. This also means octopuses tire very quickly when swimming long distances.

## There Are More Time Zones Than Countries
Earth has around 195 countries but over 24 official time zones — in fact, some regions use half-hour and quarter-hour offsets, bringing the total to 38 distinct UTC offsets. Nepal is at UTC+5:45, India at UTC+5:30, and the Chatham Islands at UTC+12:45.
