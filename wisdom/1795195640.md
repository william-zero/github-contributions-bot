## Daily Wisdom Dispatch

**Fact:** A single cloud can weigh more than a million pounds — the water droplets are so tiny and spread out that the cloud still floats, defying your every intuition about physics.

**Fact:** The word "quiz" may have been invented as a prank in 1791 when a Dublin theater owner bet he could make a nonsense word part of everyday speech overnight. He had the word chalked on walls all over the city. By morning, everyone was asking what it meant.
