# Today's Fun Facts

## The Turritopsis dohrnii — Immortal Jellyfish
The only known animal capable of reverting to its juvenile state after reaching sexual maturity,
Turritopsis dohrnii can theoretically live forever. When stressed or aging, it transforms back into
a polyp colony and starts life over again. Scientists call this process transdifferentiation. Jellyfish
solved immortality before we invented coffee.

## Honey Never Spoils
Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible.
Honey's low moisture content, acidic pH, and natural hydrogen peroxide make it inhospitable to
bacteria and microbes. The bees essentially invented canned food millennia before humanity tried.
