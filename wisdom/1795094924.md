# Daily Facts: Curious Sounds

**Fact 1:** The word "petrichor" describes the earthy smell after rain. It comes from the Greek *petra* (stone) and *ichor* (the fluid that flows through the veins of the gods). Scientists identified the actual compound responsible — geosmin — which humans can detect at concentrations as low as 5 parts per trillion.

**Fact 2:** Sperm whales communicate using patterns of clicks called codas, and different whale clans around the world have distinct dialects — like regional accents — that are passed down culturally from mother to calf.
