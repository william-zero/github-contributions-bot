# Daily Facts

## Mantis Shrimp Vision
Mantis shrimp have 16 types of photoreceptors compared to humans' 3, yet they are surprisingly bad at distinguishing colors — they process color in parallel channels rather than comparing wavelengths.

## A Flock of Flamingos
Flamingos get their pink color entirely from carotenoid pigments in the algae and crustaceans they eat. A flamingo raised without these foods would be white.
