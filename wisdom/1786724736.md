# Counterintuitive Geography

- **Australia is wider than the moon.** The moon's diameter is about 3,474 km; Australia's east-to-west span is roughly 4,000 km.
- **Finland has more lakes than any other country** — over 188,000 — yet it is not considered particularly wet; much of it is classified as subarctic.
- **The Sahara Desert is smaller than Russia.** The Sahara covers ~9.2 million km²; Russia covers 17.1 million km². Russia is also home to the world's largest sand dunes (in Yakutia), which most people have never heard of.
