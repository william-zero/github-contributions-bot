# Fun Facts: Octopus Intelligence

- Octopuses have three hearts: two pump blood to the gills, one pumps it to the rest of the body — and all three stop beating when the octopus swims, which is why they prefer crawling.
- Despite being colorblind, octopuses can change color with incredible precision using chromatophores, iridophores, and papillae in their skin — they may "see" color through photoreceptors in their skin itself.
