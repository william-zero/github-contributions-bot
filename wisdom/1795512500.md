# Facts Worth Knowing

## The Smell of Rain Has a Name
Petrichor is the earthy scent after rainfall, but there's a second smell: *geosmin*, produced by soil bacteria called Streptomyces. Humans can detect geosmin at concentrations as low as 5 parts per trillion — we're essentially bloodhounds for rain.

## Cleopatra Lived Closer to the Moon Landing Than to the Pyramids
The Great Pyramid of Giza was completed around 2560 BCE. Cleopatra was born in 69 BCE — roughly 2,500 years after the pyramid was built. The Moon landing happened in 1969 CE, only about 2,000 years after Cleopatra. The pyramids are more ancient to Cleopatra than Cleopatra is to us.
