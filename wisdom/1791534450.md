# The Ocean Is Mostly Unknown

- Only about 20% of Earth's oceans have been mapped with any real detail. The Moon's surface is better charted than our own seafloor. We've sent more humans to space than to the deepest point of the ocean (Challenger Deep at ~11km).
- The ocean produces over half of the world's oxygen — mostly from microscopic phytoplankton — making it functionally more important to your breathing than any forest.
