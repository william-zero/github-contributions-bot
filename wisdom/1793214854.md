# Fun Facts: The Mantis Shrimp's Punch and Peacock Spider's Dance

The peacock spider (Maratus volans) is only 4-5mm long, yet males perform elaborate, colorful dances to attract mates — waving their brightly patterned abdomens like tiny disco performers. Scientists have identified over 90 species, each with a unique courtship dance.

The bombardier beetle mixes two chemicals internally to produce a scalding 100°C explosion from its rear end — an audible "pop" that startles predators. It can fire this chemical cannon up to 500 times in rapid succession without harming itself.
