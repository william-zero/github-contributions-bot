# Daily Facts

**Fact 1:** A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance." This is the single most accurately named collective noun in the animal kingdom, and ornithologists are still riding that high.

**Fact 2:** The shortest war in history was between Britain and Zanzibar in 1896. It lasted between 38 and 45 minutes — shorter than most meetings scheduled for "just a quick sync."
