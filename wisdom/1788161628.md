# Peculiar Records of the Animal Kingdom

- A mantis shrimp can punch with the force of a bullet and sees 16 types of color receptors (humans have 3). Despite this visual superpower, they are believed to process color **less** accurately than humans — quantity of cones ≠ quality of perception.
- The immortal jellyfish (*Turritopsis dohrnii*) can revert to its juvenile polyp stage after reaching adulthood, theoretically cycling through its life indefinitely. Scientists call this transdifferentiation; the jellyfish calls it Monday.
