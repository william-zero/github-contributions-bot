# Daily Wisdom

## The Mpemba Effect
Hot water can sometimes freeze faster than cold water under the same conditions. Named after Erasto Mpemba, a Tanzanian student who noticed it in 1963 while making ice cream. Scientists still debate the exact mechanism — it may involve dissolved gases, convection patterns, or hydrogen bond dynamics.

## The Cocktail Party Effect
Your brain can pick out your own name from a noisy crowd even when you're not consciously listening. This selective auditory attention lets humans focus on one conversation while filtering out background noise — a feat that still challenges modern AI audio processing.
