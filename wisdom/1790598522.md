# Things That Sound Made Up But Aren't

**The "Baader-Meinhof phenomenon" has a funnier name:** It's also called the *frequency illusion* — when you notice something new, you suddenly see it everywhere. Scientists believe this is your brain desperately trying to feel clever after years of ignoring things.

**Cleopatra's needle is older than Cleopatra:** The obelisk nicknamed "Cleopatra's Needle" in New York predates Cleopatra by about 1,400 years. She just had excellent branding, and historians couldn't resist the alliteration.

**A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance":** Biology finally got one right. Other animal group names include a "murder" of crows, a "parliament" of owls, and a "bloat" of hippopotamuses, which feels deeply personal.
