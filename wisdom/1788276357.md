---
title: "The Paradox of the Heap"
date: 2026-09-01
---

If you remove one grain of sand from a heap, it's still a heap. Remove another — still a heap. But remove enough individual grains and suddenly it's not a heap anymore. At what exact point did it stop being a heap? Nobody knows. Philosophers have been arguing about this since ancient Greece and still haven't agreed.

Bees have five eyes: two big compound ones on the sides and three tiny simple ones on top called ocelli. The top three help them navigate using the sun as a compass, even on cloudy days, because they can detect polarized light patterns we can't see.
