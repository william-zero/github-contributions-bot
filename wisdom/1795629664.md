# Fun Facts: Unusual Animal Adaptations

## The Pistol Shrimp
The pistol shrimp can snap its claw so fast it creates a cavitation bubble that briefly reaches the temperature of the sun (~8,000°F). The resulting shockwave stuns or kills prey instantly — making it one of the loudest animals on Earth relative to its size.

## Tardigrades and Cryptobiosis
Tardigrades (water bears) can survive being boiled, frozen to near absolute zero, exposed to radiation 1,000x the lethal human dose, and even the vacuum of outer space. They do this by entering cryptobiosis — essentially pausing all biological processes — and can revive after decades.
