# Curious Facts About Sound

- Sound travels about 4 times faster through water (~1,480 m/s) than through air (~343 m/s) — which is why whales can communicate across entire ocean basins.
- A "sonic boom" isn't a single bang: it's a continuous cone of compressed air that travels alongside any object moving faster than sound. You only hear it as a boom when the cone sweeps past you.
- The lowest note a human can perceive (around 20 Hz) is so deep it's felt more as a vibration than actually heard. Infrasound below this threshold — produced by earthquakes and storms — can travel thousands of kilometers.
- Silence so extreme it becomes unsettling: in Microsoft's anechoic chamber, the quietest place on Earth (-20.6 dBSPL), people hear their own heartbeat, blood flow, and even the grinding of their joints within minutes.
