# Things That Exist But Shouldn't (And Yet Here We Are)

- A "butt dial" was, in retrospect, the iPhone's killer app. Pocket consciousness.
- The word "set" has 430 definitions in the Oxford English Dictionary. It has given up on meaning anything.
- Crows hold grudges for years. Somewhere there's a crow who still resents you for that thing in 2017.
- Competitive walking is an Olympic sport. Not running. Walking. The rules are specifically designed to detect when you're running by mistake.
- The shortest war in history lasted 38–45 minutes. Someone definitely woke up late that day.
