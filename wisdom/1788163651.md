# Counterintuitive Geography

- **Australia is wider than the Moon.** Australia's east-to-west span is approximately 4,000 km; the Moon's diameter is about 3,474 km. The Moon would fit inside Australia with room to spare on both sides. Please let that ruin your sense of scale.
- **The Amazon River discharges so much freshwater** that you can drink relatively fresh water from the ocean 160 km off its coast. It accounts for roughly 20% of all freshwater entering the world's oceans — an entire ocean's mood, shaped by one river.
