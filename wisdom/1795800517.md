# Fun Facts: Clocks and Calendars

- The second was originally defined as 1/86,400 of a solar day, but Earth's rotation is slowing — atomic clocks now keep "leap seconds" to stay in sync with the wobbly planet.
- The Gregorian calendar skips 3 leap years every 400 years (century years divisible by 100 but not 400 are not leap years), making the average year exactly 365.2425 days.
