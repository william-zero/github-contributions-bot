# Wednesday Wisdom

- Crows can recognize human faces and hold grudges for years. If you're rude to one, it may alert its whole community and you'll be dive-bombed for life. Moral: be kind to birds.
- The word "set" has the most definitions of any word in the English dictionary — 430 different meanings. The sentence "Set the setter's set sets" is grammatically valid (though deeply cursed).
