# Curious Facts About Time

- The shortest measurable unit of time is the **Planck time** (~5.39 × 10⁻⁴⁴ seconds), the time it takes light to travel one Planck length. Below this scale, our current physics simply breaks down.
- A **day on Venus** (one full rotation) is longer than a **year on Venus** (one orbit around the Sun). You'd wait 243 Earth days for a single sunrise — and the Sun would rise in the west.
