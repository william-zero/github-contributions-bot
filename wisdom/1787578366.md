# Weekend Wisdom

- Butterflies taste with their feet — they have taste sensors on their tarsi to detect if a leaf is edible.
- A group of flamingos is called a flamboyance, and they're only pink because of the carotenoid pigments in the shrimp and algae they eat.
- Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
