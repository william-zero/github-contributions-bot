# Weekend Wisdom: The Art of Doing Nothing

Did you know that sea otters hold hands while sleeping to keep from drifting apart? 
They call this a "raft" — a group of otters floating together, paws intertwined, 
napping on the ocean surface. It's one of the most adorable survival strategies in nature.

Meanwhile, on land, the humble sloth sleeps up to 20 hours a day. Scientists once thought 
this was pure laziness, but it turns out sleeping is a key energy-conservation strategy — 
their leafy diet provides so few calories that resting is essentially a superpower.

Sometimes the best action is restful stillness.
