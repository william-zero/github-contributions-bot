# Fun Facts for Today

**The Mantis Shrimp's Punch**: The mantis shrimp can strike with the speed of a bullet — about 23 meters per second — generating forces up to 1,500 Newtons. The strike is so fast it creates cavitation bubbles that implode with their own shockwave, effectively hitting prey twice per strike.

**Cleopatra and the Moon Landing**: Cleopatra VII lived closer in time to the Moon landing (1969) than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza (~2560 BCE). She was born around 69 BCE, making the pyramid about 2,500 years old in her era — but only ~2,000 years separated her from Neil Armstrong.
