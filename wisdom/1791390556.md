# Miscellaneous Marvels

- Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza. The pyramids were already 2,500 years old when she was born.
- The total weight of all ants on Earth is roughly equal to the total weight of all humans on Earth. There are estimated to be 20 quadrillion ants. They are watching. They are waiting.
