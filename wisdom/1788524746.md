# Daily Wisdom

## The Sound of Silence Has a Frequency

In an anechoic chamber — a room engineered to absorb 99.99% of sound — most people start hearing their own heartbeat, blood flowing, and even the electrical hum of their nervous system within minutes. The quietest place on Earth is so silent that it becomes uncomfortable; humans are not built for true silence.

## Every Proton Has Survived Since the Big Bang

The protons in your body are approximately 13.8 billion years old. They formed in the first few minutes after the Big Bang and have survived every star, supernova, and geological epoch since. You are not just made of stardust — you are carrying particles that predate every star that has ever existed.
