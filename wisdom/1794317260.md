# The Quiet Science of Naps

Humans are one of the few mammals that consolidate sleep into a single long block. Most mammals nap throughout the day — your dog is actually doing it right.

A 10–20 minute nap boosts alertness without entering deep sleep, so you wake up refreshed instead of groggy. NASA pilots who napped 40 minutes improved performance by 34% and alertness by 100%.

The word "siesta" comes from the Latin *hora sexta* — the sixth hour after dawn, roughly noon — the perfect time the ancient Romans built into their schedules for a midday rest.
