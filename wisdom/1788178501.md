# Did You Know?

The mantis shrimp can punch with the force of a bullet — its strike accelerates at 10,000g,
fast enough to cavitate the water around it and generate flashes of light from the collapsing
bubbles. It also sees 16 types of color receptors compared to humans' 3.

Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that
was still perfectly edible. The low moisture content and acidic pH create an inhospitable
environment for bacteria and microorganisms.
