# Daily Curiosities

## The Shortest War in History
The Anglo-Zanzibar War of 1896 lasted between 38 and 45 minutes, making it the shortest war in recorded history. Zanzibar surrendered after a brief naval bombardment, and casualties were surprisingly light on the British side (zero), though hundreds were wounded or killed on the Zanzibari side.

## Crows Hold Grudges
Ravens and crows not only recognize individual human faces but will remember people who have wronged them for years — and will recruit other crows to mob that specific person. If you upset a crow in a city, the local crow community may remember your face for the rest of their lives.
