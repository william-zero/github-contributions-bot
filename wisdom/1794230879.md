# Fun Facts: Optical Illusions of Nature

- Flamingos are actually white — their pink color comes entirely from carotenoid pigments in the algae and crustaceans they eat. A flamingo raised without that diet would be completely white.
- The sky on Mars appears butterscotch-orange during the day and briefly turns blue at sunrise and sunset — the exact opposite of Earth's sky — due to iron oxide dust scattering light differently.
