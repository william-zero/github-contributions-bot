# Mantis Shrimp Vision

Mantis shrimp have 16 types of photoreceptors (humans have 3), yet they are surprisingly
bad at distinguishing colors. Scientists believe their visual system is optimized for
speed rather than color resolution — they identify colors by comparing photoreceptors
directly instead of blending signals, making them faster but less discerning.

Bonus: they can punch with the force of a bullet and see UV, infrared, and circularly
polarized light. Absolute overkill for a crustacean.
