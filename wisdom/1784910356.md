# Curious Creatures of the Deep

**The Mantis Shrimp Sees More Colors Than You**
Humans have 3 types of color receptors (cones) in their eyes. The mantis shrimp has 16. But oddly, research suggests they may actually be *worse* at distinguishing similar colors — instead of mixing signals like we do, they seem to identify colors using a quick on/off system, like a spectral barcode scanner.

**Jellyfish Are Older Than Trees**
Jellyfish have been swimming Earth's oceans for at least 500 million years, predating trees by roughly 100 million years. Some species are even considered biologically immortal, able to revert to their juvenile polyp stage after reaching adulthood.
