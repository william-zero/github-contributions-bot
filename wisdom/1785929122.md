# Curious Facts About Bees

- A single honeybee will produce only about 1/12 teaspoon of honey in its entire lifetime, yet a hive collectively visits around 2 million flowers to make just one pound of honey.
- Bees can recognize human faces using the same pattern-recognition techniques that we do, making them one of the few insects known to have this ability.
- The queen bee can lay up to 2,000 eggs per day — more than her own body weight — and lives for up to five years, far outlasting the average worker bee's lifespan of just six weeks.
