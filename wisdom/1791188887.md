# Daily Facts - Oct 5

## Fact 1: Octopuses Have Three Hearts
An octopus has three hearts: two pump blood through the gills, and one pumps it through the body. When an octopus swims, the heart that delivers blood to the body actually stops beating — which is why octopuses prefer crawling. Basically, they get winded by their own cardiovascular system.

## Fact 2: The Most Isolated Tree in the World
Until a drunk driver hit it in 1973, the Tree of Ténéré in Niger was considered the most isolated tree on Earth — the only tree within 400 km (250 miles) in the Sahara Desert. It was used as a navigation landmark by caravans. A lone acacia, completely alone in a vast desert, outlasting centuries... until a truck.
