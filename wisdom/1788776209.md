# The Banach-Tarski Paradox

The Banach-Tarski paradox states that a solid ball can theoretically be decomposed into a finite number of pieces and reassembled into two balls of identical size.

This isn't a physical trick — it relies on the Axiom of Choice and non-measurable sets that can't exist in physical space. The "pieces" are infinitely complex and have no well-defined volume.

The paradox highlights how mathematical infinity operates by different rules than physical intuition. A consequence: volume is not conserved under arbitrary decomposition.
