# Fun Facts for Today

## The Baader-Meinhof Phenomenon
Ever learn a new word and then suddenly hear it everywhere? That's the Baader-Meinhof phenomenon (also called frequency illusion). Your brain starts selectively noticing something it previously filtered out, making it seem like the universe is conspiring to show you that thing constantly.

## Chickens Dream
Chickens enter REM sleep, meaning they likely experience dreams. Researchers have observed rapid eye movements and temporary muscle paralysis in sleeping chickens — the same markers of dreaming sleep in humans. What do chickens dream about? Probably corn.
