# Fun Facts: Strange Animal Superpowers

- Mantis shrimp can punch with the force of a bullet (accelerating their clubs at 10,000 g) and also see 16 types of color receptors — humans have only 3.
- Tardigrades (water bears) can survive in the vacuum of space, withstand radiation doses 1,000 times lethal to humans, and go without water for 30 years by entering cryptobiosis.
