# Weekend Fact: The Mantis Shrimp's Vision

Mantis shrimp have 16 types of photoreceptors in their eyes, compared to just 3 in humans.
Despite this, research suggests they may actually be worse at distinguishing between similar colors —
they process visual information differently, using a scanning system rather than pure spectral comparison.
It's a reminder that more complexity doesn't always mean better perception; it just means different.
