# Fun Facts: Honey and Lightning

## Honey Never Expires
Archaeologists have found honey in ancient Egyptian tombs that is over 3,000 years old and still perfectly edible. Honey's low moisture content and slightly acidic pH create an inhospitable environment for bacteria and microorganisms. The bees even add an enzyme that produces hydrogen peroxide as a preservative.

## Lightning Strikes the Earth 100 Times Per Second
On average, lightning strikes Earth about 100 times every single second — that's roughly 8 million lightning strikes per day. Each bolt can reach temperatures around 30,000 Kelvin (about 5 times hotter than the surface of the sun) and travels at roughly 270,000 mph. Despite this, the total energy in a single lightning bolt could only power a 100-watt light bulb for about 3 months.
