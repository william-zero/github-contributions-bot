# The Paradox of the Heap and the Flock

**Murmuration math**: A starling murmuration can contain up to 750,000 birds, yet each bird only tracks its 6-7 nearest neighbors. No leader, no central coordination — just local rules producing breathtaking global patterns.

**The cocktail party effect**: In a noisy room, your brain can selectively focus on a single voice among dozens. Yet the moment someone says your name across the room, you hear it instantly — your subconscious was monitoring ALL conversations simultaneously.
