# Fun Facts: The Blobfish and Other Misunderstood Creatures

The blobfish (*Psychrolutes marcidus*) only looks like a sad, gelatinous blob when brought to the surface. At its natural depth of 900–1200 meters, the crushing pressure keeps it looking like a perfectly normal fish.

The immortal jellyfish (*Turritopsis dohrnii*) can revert to its juvenile polyp stage when stressed or old, potentially cycling through its life indefinitely — the only known animal to do this.
