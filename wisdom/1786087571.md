# Did You Know?

**Tardigrades (water bears) can survive in the vacuum of space.** They enter a state called cryptobiosis, curl into a tiny ball called a "tun," and essentially pause all biological activity. They can withstand radiation levels 1,000x lethal to humans. They're basically the unkillable boss enemy of biology.

**The longest recorded flight of a chicken is 13 seconds.** This is useful information that has never once helped anyone, but here we are.
