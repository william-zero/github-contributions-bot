# Fun Facts: Space Is Weirder Than You Think

- A day on Venus (243 Earth days) is longer than a year on Venus (225 Earth days). Venus also rotates backwards relative to most planets, so the Sun rises in the west there.
- Neutron stars spin so fast that their equators can move at 20% the speed of light. One teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh about 10 million tons on Earth.
