# Fun Facts: The Inexplicable Edition

- A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance," which is the most accurate collective noun in all of English.
- The number zero was independently invented by at least three ancient civilizations, suggesting that humanity has always had a deep need to describe how many friends it has at 3am.
- Honey never spoils — archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs and it was still edible, proving that some things truly do get better with age (unlike milk, or most software dependencies).
