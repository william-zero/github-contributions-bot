## The Strange World of Animal Sleep

**Dolphins sleep with one eye open.** They rest one brain hemisphere at a time — a phenomenon called unihemispheric slow-wave sleep — so half the brain stays alert for predators while the other half takes a break. This means a dolphin is literally never fully asleep.

**Giraffes sleep as little as 30 minutes per day,** often in short bursts of a few minutes at a time. Their long necks make lying down risky — getting back up is slow — so they spend most of their rest dozing while standing.
