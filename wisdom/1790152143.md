# Facts That Sound Made Up But Aren't

- A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance," which is the most accurate collective noun in existence.
- The dot above a lowercase 'i' or 'j' is called a **tittle**. Losing your tittle is surprisingly undramatic.
- Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
- Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. Let that one marinate for a moment.
