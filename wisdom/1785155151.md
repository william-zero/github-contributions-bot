# Delightfully Useless Facts

## The Mantis Shrimp's Superpower

Mantis shrimps can punch with the speed of a bullet (roughly 23 m/s), generating cavitation bubbles that briefly reach the temperature of the sun's surface. They also see 16 types of color receptors compared to humans' measly 3 — yet scientists believe they process color *worse* than we do. Having superpowers doesn't mean you use them wisely.

## Cleopatra Lived Closer to the Moon Landing Than to the Pyramids

The Great Pyramid of Giza was built around 2560 BCE. Cleopatra was born around 69 BCE — roughly 2,500 years after the pyramids. The Moon landing was in 1969 CE, only about 2,000 years after Cleopatra. Time is extremely rude about where it puts things.
