# Today's Fascinating Facts

## The Shortest War in History
The Anglo-Zanzibar War of 1896 lasted between 38 and 45 minutes — making it the shortest recorded war in history. Zanzibar surrendered before the British had even finished their morning tea.

## Honey Never Expires
Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Honey's low moisture content and acidic pH make it inhospitable to bacteria, essentially making it immortal food.
