# Fun Facts: Time, Sound, and Surprising Records

- A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus — it takes 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.
- The loudest sound ever recorded in human history was the 1883 eruption of Krakatoa, heard clearly over 4,800 km (3,000 miles) away in Australia.
- Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire — teaching began there around 1096 CE, while the Aztec civilization rose in the 14th century.
