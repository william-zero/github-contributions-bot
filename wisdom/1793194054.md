# Fun Facts: The Mantis Shrimp

The mantis shrimp has 16 types of photoreceptors in its eyes, compared to just 3 in humans — yet scientists believe it actually processes color *less* efficiently than we do, using a unique parallel system rather than comparing wavelengths.

Their club-like appendages can strike with the speed of a bullet (about 23 m/s), creating cavitation bubbles that briefly reach the temperature of the surface of the sun.
