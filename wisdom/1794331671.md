# The Curious Physics of Boiling Water

- Water doesn't actually boil at 100°C everywhere — at high altitudes, lower air pressure means it boils at lower temperatures. On Mount Everest, water boils around 70°C, which is why instant noodles are always a bit undercooked up there.
- Superheated water can exist as a liquid above 100°C if it's very pure and the container is very smooth — disturbing it can cause explosive boiling. This is why microwave warnings say not to heat plain water too long.
