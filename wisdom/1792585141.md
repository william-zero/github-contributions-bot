# Fun Facts: Tardigrades and Dolphins

## Tardigrades Are Basically Indestructible
Tardigrades (water bears) can survive the vacuum of outer space, temperatures from -272°C to 150°C, radiation doses 1,000x lethal to humans, and even the pressure of deep ocean trenches. They achieve this by entering a state called cryptobiosis — essentially pressing pause on all biological processes until conditions improve.

## Dolphins Call Each Other by Name
Dolphins develop unique "signature whistles" early in life that function like names. Other dolphins copy a companion's signature whistle to get their attention, and dolphins remember the whistles of individuals they haven't seen for over 20 years. It's one of the most sophisticated communication systems known outside of humans.
