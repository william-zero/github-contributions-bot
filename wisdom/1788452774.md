# Curious Facts: Numbers & Patterns

- The number 1729 is called the Hardy-Ramanujan number — it's the smallest number expressible as the sum of two cubes in two different ways: 1³+12³ and 9³+10³.
- If you shuffle a deck of cards perfectly randomly, the exact order has almost certainly never existed before in the history of the universe — the number of possible orderings is 52! (about 8×10⁶⁷).
