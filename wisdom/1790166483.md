# Weird But True: The Universe Is Mostly Nothing

- About 99.9999999999996% of an atom is empty space. If you removed all the empty space from every atom in every human being on Earth, all of humanity would fit in a sugar cube.
- The Sun makes up 99.86% of the mass of the entire Solar System. Everything else — all 8 planets, moons, asteroids, comets — is the remaining 0.14%.
- There are more possible iterations of a game of chess than there are atoms in the observable universe. The number starts with a 1 and has 120 digits after it.
