# Fun Facts: Remarkable Animal Architects

**Bower birds are the interior designers of the animal kingdom.** Male bowerbirds construct elaborate structures called "bowers" — decorated with colorful objects like berries, flowers, shells, and even discarded bottle caps — not to live in, but purely to impress females. After mating, the female flies off to build a completely separate nest on her own.

**Termite mounds are natural skyscrapers with built-in air conditioning.** African termite mounds can reach 9 meters tall and maintain an internal temperature of 30°C regardless of outside conditions, achieved through a sophisticated network of ventilation shafts the termites actively open and close throughout the day.
