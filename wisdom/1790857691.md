# Today's Fun Facts

**The Baader-Meinhof Phenomenon:** Once you learn a new word or concept, you suddenly start seeing it everywhere. It's not that it's actually appearing more — your brain has just been told to flag it. You were always surrounded by "semantic soulmates" but your brain was ghosting them.

**Competitive Thumb-Twiddling:** Yes, this is a real sport. The World Thumb Wrestling Federation holds annual championships where participants lock hands and try to pin each other's thumbs. The fastest recorded pinning time is 0.22 seconds — quicker than a human blink.
