# Strange But True

**Octopus arms have minds of their own.** Two-thirds of an octopus's neurons live in its arms, not its brain — each arm can taste, touch, and make movement decisions independently. An arm can continue reacting for up to an hour after being severed.

**Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid.** The pyramid was built around 2560 BCE; Cleopatra died in 30 BCE; the Moon landing was 1969 CE. Cleopatra-to-Moon: ~2000 years. Pyramid-to-Cleopatra: ~2500 years.
