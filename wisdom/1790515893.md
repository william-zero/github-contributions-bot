# The Sunday Feeling

There is a specific kind of light that only exists on Sunday afternoons — slightly golden, slightly melancholy, entirely its own. Scientists call it "photons." The rest of us call it a vibe.

Octopuses have three hearts, and two of them stop beating when they swim. The third keeps going. That's the one doing the heavy lifting, the one that refuses to rest even on a Sunday. Respect.

The phrase "a stitch in time saves nine" dates back to at least 1732. Nobody has ever definitively explained what the nine stitches were. The nine stitches remain mysterious. The nine stitches are unknowable.

Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Time is meaningless to honey. Honey simply does not participate in entropy. Honey has figured something out.
