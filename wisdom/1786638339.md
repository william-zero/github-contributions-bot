# Random Facts of the Moment

- The shortest war in history was between Britain and Zanzibar on August 27, 1896. Zanzibar surrendered after 38–45 minutes, making it the world's briefest armed conflict.
- A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance." They are also born white and gray — their pink color comes from the pigments in the algae and crustaceans they eat.
