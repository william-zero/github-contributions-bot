# Fun Facts of the Day

## The Immortal Jellyfish
*Turritopsis dohrnii* can revert to its juvenile polyp stage after reaching full maturity — effectively cheating death by going backwards through its life cycle. It's the only known animal capable of doing this repeatedly, making it biologically immortal. Scientists call this process transdifferentiation.

## Honey Never Expires
Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Honey's low moisture content and slightly acidic pH create an inhospitable environment for bacteria and microorganisms — a natural preservative that outlasts empires.
