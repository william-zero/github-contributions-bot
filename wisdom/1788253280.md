# Today's Wisdom

## The Mpemba Effect
Hot water can freeze faster than cold water under certain conditions — a phenomenon called the Mpemba effect. First documented by a Tanzanian student named Erasto Mpemba in 1963, it has puzzled scientists ever since. The exact mechanism is still debated, with proposed explanations ranging from dissolved gases to convection currents.

## Octopus Blood
Octopuses have blue blood because they use hemocyanin (copper-based) instead of hemoglobin (iron-based) to carry oxygen. Hemocyanin is less efficient than hemoglobin at oxygen transport but works better in cold, low-oxygen environments — perfect for deep-sea life.
