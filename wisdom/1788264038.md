# Strange But True

## The Paradox of the Ship of Theseus
If you replace every plank of a ship one by one until none of the original wood remains, is it still the same ship? Greek philosophers argued about this for centuries. Modern humans argue about it at 2am on Reddit.

## Honey Never Spoils
Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Bees are basically running a long-term food storage operation that makes human preppers look like amateurs.
