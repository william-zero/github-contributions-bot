## Fun Facts for Today

**The Turritopsis dohrnii jellyfish** is biologically immortal — when stressed, it can revert to its juvenile polyp stage and start aging again from scratch. It's the only known animal that can repeatedly cheat death this way.

**Crows remember faces.** They can recognize specific humans, hold grudges for years, and even teach their offspring which faces to distrust. Getting on a crow's bad side is a multigenerational commitment.
