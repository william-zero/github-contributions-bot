# Fun Facts: Surprisingly Clever Animals

- Crows can recognize individual human faces, hold grudges for years, and even bring gifts to people who feed them regularly.
- Elephants are one of the few animals that pass the mirror test, recognizing their own reflections — and they mourn their dead.
- Octopuses have three hearts, blue blood, and distributed intelligence: each arm has its own mini-brain and can act semi-independently.
