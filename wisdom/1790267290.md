# Today in Weird History

## The Great Molasses Flood of 1919
On January 15, 1919, a 2.3-million-gallon tank of molasses burst in Boston's North End, sending a wave of sticky doom
through the streets at an estimated 35 mph. Buildings were swept off their foundations. The harbor stayed sweet for
months. Witnesses reported the air smelled of molasses for years afterward.

## Competitive Endurance Standing
The world record for standing still is held by a man who stood motionless for 24 hours straight in 2008. This is
considered a sport in some circles. His strategy: "I tried not to think about anything." Experts remain baffled that
this constitutes a world record.
