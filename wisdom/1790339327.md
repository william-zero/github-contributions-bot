## Why Bananas Are Slightly Radioactive

Bananas contain potassium-40, a naturally occurring radioactive isotope. This is so
common that radiation exposure from food is measured in "banana equivalent doses" (BED).

Eating a banana: 1 BED (0.1 microsieverts).
Living next to a nuclear plant for a year: ~52 BED.
A chest X-ray: ~400 BED.
Smoking a pack a day for a year: ~36,000 BED.

The most radioactive thing in most kitchens is the fruit bowl.
