# Things That Sound Impossible But Are Physics

- If you took all the DNA in a single human body and stretched it end to end, it would reach from Earth to the Sun and back roughly 300 times. The Sun is about 93 million miles away.
- Every second, the Sun converts about 4 million tons of matter directly into energy via E=mc². That energy takes around 100,000 years to travel from the core to the surface, then about 8 minutes to reach your face.
- Light from distant galaxies that you can see with the naked eye left those galaxies before Earth existed. You are looking at the past with your own eyes, right now.
