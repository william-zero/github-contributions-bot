# Today's Fun Facts

## The Banach-Tarski Paradox
In mathematics, it's theoretically possible to decompose a sphere into a finite number of pieces and reassemble them into *two* spheres of the same size — just by rearranging the pieces without stretching. This relies on the Axiom of Choice and works only in abstract math, not physical reality. Still: two spheres from one!

## Honey Never Spoils
Archaeologists have found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that was still perfectly edible. Honey's low moisture content, acidic pH, and natural hydrogen peroxide make it essentially inhospitable to bacteria indefinitely.
