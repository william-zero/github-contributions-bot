# Daily Wisdom

## The Zeigarnik Effect
People remember uncompleted tasks far better than completed ones. Soviet psychologist Bluma Zeigarnik discovered this while noticing that waiters could recall unpaid orders perfectly but forgot settled tabs immediately. This is why cliffhangers work, and why you can't stop thinking about that one email you haven't replied to.

## The Streisand Effect
Attempting to suppress or censor information online often causes it to spread far more widely than it would have otherwise. Named after Barbra Streisand, who in 2003 tried to suppress aerial photos of her California home — resulting in 420,000 downloads of the very photos she wanted hidden.
