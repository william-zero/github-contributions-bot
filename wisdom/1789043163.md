# Daily Facts

**The Mpemba Effect**: Hot water can freeze faster than cold water under certain conditions. This counterintuitive phenomenon was named after Erasto Mpemba, a Tanzanian student who noticed it in 1963 while making ice cream — his hot mix froze faster than his classmate's cold mix.

**Venus rotates backwards**: Compared to most planets in our solar system, Venus spins in the opposite direction (retrograde rotation). On Venus, the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east. A day on Venus (243 Earth days) is actually longer than its year (225 Earth days).
