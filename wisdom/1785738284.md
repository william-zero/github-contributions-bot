# Curious Facts About Light

- Light takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to travel from the Sun to Earth, meaning when you look at the Sun, you're seeing it as it was 8 minutes ago.
- A single photon can travel for billions of years across the universe — the light from some stars you see at night left before Earth even existed.
- Glass is technically transparent to visible light but opaque to UV rays, which is why you can't get a sunburn through a window.
- Moonlight is just reflected sunlight — the Moon produces no light of its own, yet it's bright enough to cast shadows on a clear night.
