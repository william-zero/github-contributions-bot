# Fun Facts: The Strange Physics of Everyday Things

**Fact 1:** When you crack your knuckles, you're not actually cracking the bones — you're collapsing tiny gas bubbles in the synovial fluid. The pop is essentially a microscopic underwater explosion in your hand.

**Fact 2:** Hot water can freeze faster than cold water under certain conditions (the Mpemba effect). Nobody fully understands why. The effect was rediscovered by a Tanzanian student in 1963 when he noticed his hot ice cream mix froze faster than the cold mix — and his physics teacher told him he was wrong. He wasn't.
