# The Paradox of the Heap

The ancient Greeks puzzled over this: if you remove one grain of sand from a heap, is it still a heap? Keep removing grains — at what point does it stop being a heap? This is the Sorites paradox, applied to almost everything in life. Categories like "bald," "tall," or "old" have no precise cutoff, yet we use them every day without confusion.

## Bonus: The Bootstrap Paradox

If you go back in time and give someone the original idea for a book, and they write it, and you read it, and then go back to give them the idea... where did the idea originally come from? Some information seems to exist in loops with no origin point, which either breaks causality or suggests reality is stranger than we imagined.
