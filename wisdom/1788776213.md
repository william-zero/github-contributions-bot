# The Paradox of the Heap

The ancient Greeks puzzled over this: if you remove one grain of sand from a heap, is it still a heap? Keep removing grains one by one — at what point does it stop being a heap? There's no clear boundary. This "Sorites paradox" reveals that many everyday concepts (bald, tall, old) have fuzzy edges, yet we use them without confusion because context does the heavy lifting.

## The Bootstrap Paradox

If a time traveler goes back and gives Beethoven the sheet music for his Fifth Symphony (which the traveler learned from recordings of Beethoven's own work), where did the symphony originally come from? Some information loops seem to have no originating cause — either causality breaks, or certain things exist without ever being created.
