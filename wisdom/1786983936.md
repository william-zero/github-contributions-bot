# Things That Are Surprisingly Old

- The recipe for beer is over 5,000 years old — Sumerian clay tablets describe it in detail
- Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire (teaching started ~1096 AD; Tenochtitlán founded 1325 AD)
- The fax machine was invented before the telephone (1843 vs 1876)
- Nintendo was founded in 1889 — as a playing card company
- France was still executing people by guillotine in 1977, the year Star Wars came out
